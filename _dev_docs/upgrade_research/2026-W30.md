**VRAM:** 13 GB peak (fits RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB)
**License:** NSCLv1 (non-commercial only)

Comfy-Org hosts weights at `Comfy-Org/PixelDiT` (198 K downloads, 133 likes as of 2026-07-22).
