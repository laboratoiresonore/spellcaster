## Tier 1 — Drop-in supersessions (ship soon)

### 1. LTX-2.5 → `build_ltx_video`

**Released:** 2026-08-11 (weights on HuggingFace) / ComfyUI v0.32.0 same day.

**What changed vs LTX-Video 2.x:**
- New Diffusion Fidelity Rendering: allocates compute by scene complexity; generates structure in 8× temporally compressed latent space + keyframes, then a pixel-diffusion stage renders final output.
- Native multi-shot generation — one model, multiple shots in sequence.
- New video decoder, custom text encoder, improved distilled variant.
- Synchronized audio output (paired video+audio generation).
- 4K video resolution support.

**VRAM:** 16 GB minimum (meets RTX 5060 Ti budget). Full-quality 32 GB (skips most of the features above).

**ComfyUI changes needed:**
- Update to ComfyUI v0.32.0+ (adds `LTXVSTGGuider`, `LTXVModalityGuider`, `LTXVDualCFGGuider`, `LTXVDurationPredictor` nodes).
- Download `Lightricks/LTX-2.5` weights.
- Adjust `build_ltx_video` to wire the new nodes (STG, Dual-CFG guiders).

**Source:** https://hf.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5  
**Risk:** medium — new decoder architecture; float64 device bug was patched post-v0.32.0 tag (check master).
